# MENA Organization Knowledge Base

## Organization Overview

**MENA (Middle East and North Africa Engineering Education and Training Organization)** is Jordan's first non-profit organization dedicated to education, training, innovation, and space-related activities. MENA focuses on advancing STEM education, space exploration, technology, healthcare, sustainability, climate solutions, agriculture, and engineering across the MENA region.

The organization serves students, educators, researchers, professionals, and institutions by providing training programs, workshops, analog missions, research opportunities, and international collaborations.

MENA has engaged more than 150 students and young professionals from across the MENA region in its projects and initiatives.

# MENA Analog Mission Program

## MENA-00-WR

### Overview

MENA-00-WR is the first analog Mars mission led by MENA.

### Mission Dates

October 24–29, 2025

### Location

Wadi Rum, Jordan

### Purpose

To simulate life and operations on Mars in a realistic desert environment that closely resembles Martian terrain.

### Habitat

* First analog mission habitat of its kind in the Arab region
* Designed and operated by MENA
* Modular dome architecture
* Solar-powered systems
* Active habitat supporting mission crews

### Mission Components

* Astronaut Crew
* Mission Control Center (MCC)
* Extra-Vehicular Activities (EVAs)
* Scientific Experiments
* Engineering Operations

### Mission Control Center (MCC)

The MCC supports astronauts throughout the mission by:

* Monitoring operations
* Coordinating activities
* Managing communications
* Ensuring mission safety

# SYSTEM Program

## Space for Youth through Science, Technology, Engineering, and Medicine (SYSTEM)

### Overview

SYSTEM is an educational initiative developed by MENA to introduce school students to space exploration while connecting STEM disciplines to real-world challenges.

### Target Audience

Students in Grades 6–11.

### Objectives

* Promote STEM learning
* Inspire future innovators
* Develop teamwork and problem-solving skills
* Demonstrate how science, engineering, technology, and medicine support space exploration

### Program Structure

* 20 interactive sessions per year
* Hands-on experiments
* Team projects
* Creative challenges
* Space-themed STEM activities
